How it works

From address to cash, without the hard parts.

No listings, no repairs, no waiting on a buyer's loan. Here's exactly what happens from the moment you reach out to the day you get paid.

1

Tell us about your house

Share your address and a few quick details about the property — its condition, your situation, and the timeline you have in mind. It takes about two minutes online, or you can call a specialist directly.

About 2 minutes · no obligation
2

We review and reach out

A local Florida specialist studies recent comparable sales in your neighborhood and the home's condition. If anything's unclear, we'll call to ask — friendly questions, never a sales pitch.

A real person who knows your market
3

Get your fair cash offer

Usually within 24 hours, you'll receive a clear, all-cash offer with no fees attached. We'll walk you through exactly how we arrived at the number so it never feels like a black box.

Usually within 24 hours · $0 in fees

Close on the date you choose

Accept when you're ready and pick a closing date — as soon as 7 days or months down the road. We handle the paperwork and closing costs, and you get paid. That's it.

As few as 7 days, or whenever suits you
