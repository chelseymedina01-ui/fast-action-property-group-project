About us

A Florida team that treats sellers like neighbors.

Fast Action Property Group was built on a simple belief: selling a house shouldn't be the hardest part of a hard season. We're here to make it the easy one.

Our story

Started in 2018, in our own backyard.

We started Fast Action after watching friends and family struggle to sell homes the traditional way — repairs they couldn't afford, showings that dragged on, buyers who backed out at the last minute.

So we built the company we wished they'd had: a local buyer who shows up with a fair cash offer, no fees, and a closing date that bends to your life. Eight years later, we've helped more than 500 Florida families move forward.
